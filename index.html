01 / About

Product, partnerships and the compliance work in between.

I lead product at Tesseract, a MiCA-licensed crypto yield provider. I own two B2B products there: institutional vaults and a yield API.

I like B2B work best: our clients build on our product. The licence shapes what we can build, so I work as closely with compliance and legal as with engineering.

Before that I ran partnerships at Bitstamp for three years, including Stripe. They kept pulling me into product decisions, and I wanted to own them.

Before crypto, I spent four years at Sofico turning client needs into software specs. That's where I found I enjoy product work. Outside work, I build tools to pick DeFi investments and manage their risk.
