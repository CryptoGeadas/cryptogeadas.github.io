01 / About

Product, partnerships and the compliance work in between.

I've used crypto since 2017 and worked in it since 2021. Today I lead product at Tesseract, a MiCA-licensed crypto yield provider.

I like building what other companies run on. At a licensed provider the licence shapes what you can build, so I work as closely with compliance and legal as with engineering.

Before that I spent three years in partnerships at Bitstamp. The partnerships kept pulling me into product decisions, and I wanted to own them.
