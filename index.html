01 / About

Product, partnerships and the compliance work in between.

I'm a Lead Product Manager based in Lisbon. I've used crypto since 2017 and worked in it since 2021, first in partnerships at Bitstamp, now leading product at Tesseract, a MiCA-licensed crypto yield provider.

I like building what other companies run on. At Tesseract I own the roadmaps for our institutional vaults and for Earn API, the B2B yield API that exchanges and fintechs build into their own products. Together with compliance, I set up the KYC, AML and Travel Rule flows behind them.

At Bitstamp I took partnerships from the first call to go-to-market, including the one behind Stripe's fiat-to-crypto onramp in the EU, and Mastercard as a deposit method.

Before crypto, I spent four years at Sofico turning client needs into software specs. That's where I found I like product work. Outside work, I build small data tools for questions I have as a DeFi user.
