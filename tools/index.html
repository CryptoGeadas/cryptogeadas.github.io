How I build

Same pattern, every tool.

  1. 01

    Grill the idea

    Before any code, I question the idea hard: who decides what with it, and what it has to beat to be worth using.

  2. 02

    Prototype on real data

    A throwaway probe on live data with a go/no-go test. Ideas that fail the test stop here.

  3. 03

    Ship small and automated

    Public data goes through a scheduled job and a static site. Tools that need your own data run on your computer. Everything is open source.
