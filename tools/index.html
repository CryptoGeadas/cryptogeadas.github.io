DeFi Yield Scanner

"Where can I park my stables right now, and is that yield real?"

A trust filter, not a yield hunter. It screens every stablecoin pool DefiLlama tracks against a hand-kept list of trusted protocols and stablecoins, then ranks by durable, organic yield. A high APY counts as a red flag as often as an opportunity.

/tokenized-rwa-tracker/● LIVE

Tokenized RWA Tracker

"I want to park euros or dollars in real-world yield. Show me what's real, what's risky and what's fake."

Classifies tokenized funds by what actually backs them (government debt, private credit, basis trades and more), gives each a trust tier, and flags products whose label doesn't match what they hold. New products above $5M TVL surface automatically as "unclassified" until reviewed.

Crypto Fundamentals

"What am I actually buying?"

Type a ticker and see a token's fundamentals in one place: valuation against its peers, traction, value accrual, dilution and unlocks, holder concentration, treasury, security and development activity. 03 / Shelved on purpose

Prototyped on real data, then stopped

Correlation Heatmap

Shelved

The idea: find crypto assets that diversify away from BTC. On a year of real data, the only things that did were stablecoins and gold. Every risk asset sat between 0.66 and 0.92 correlation.

Verdict

The tool's only honest answer was "hold cash", which isn't worth building a tool for.

Token Unlock vs Price

Shelved

The idea: a calendar of upcoming unlocks with the expected price reaction. On 22,168 historical unlock events, the reaction turned out to be the token's normal drift, not the unlock. What was left was about as predictive as a coin flip.

Verdict

No tool, but the finding became the research piece above.

How I build

Same pattern, every tool.

  1. 01

    Grill the idea

    Before any code, I question the idea hard: who decides what with it, and what it has to beat to be worth using.

  2. 02

    Prototype on real data

    A throwaway probe on live data with a go/no-go test. Two of the four ideas so far stopped here.

  3. 03

    Ship small and automated

    A scheduled job pulls DefiLlama, a pure pipeline applies the opinion, and a static site publishes it. Everything is open source.
